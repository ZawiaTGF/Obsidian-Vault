### 1. The 2-Hour Validation (Pre-Validation & Research)

The goal of this phase is to quickly filter through multiple ideas (e.g., assessing five different ideas in 10 hours) to see which ones warrant a closer look.

- **The 5PM Framework:** Evaluate core aspects of the idea, including the _Problem, Purchaser, Pricing model, Market, Product-founder fit,_ and the _Pain_ being solved.
- **SEO & Keyword Research:** Check tools and online communities (Twitter, Reddit, Facebook groups) to see if people are already publicly discussing the pain point.
- **Distribution Assessment:** If people aren’t searching for the problem, realize you will have to rely heavily on cold outreach later to find them.
### 2. The 20-Hour Validation (Conversations & Landing Pages)

This step is about getting 11 to 40 "yeses" from target users promising they would use or try the solution. This can be achieved through two primary methods:

- **Conversations (Warm & Cold):** Reach out to your network or do cold outreach on platforms like LinkedIn to talk to potential ideal customers. You can even offer to pay their hourly consulting rate to pick their brain. _(Note: If you have an established audience, be careful of the "curse of the audience," where followers say they like an idea just to be supportive).
- **Landing Pages:** Build a simple page communicating your value proposition with an email capture widget. Figure out exactly how you would drive traffic to it (using strategies from books like _The SaaS Playbook_ or _Traction_). Use the 20 or 30 emails you collect to start direct dialogues about their pain points and what they would pay.
### 3. The 200-Hour Validation (Building the MVP)

Only move to this phase after completing the 2 and 20-hour steps.

- Spend approximately 200 hours putting a Minimum Viable Product (MVP) into the hands of users to kickstart the continuous iteration cycle.
- This phase doesn't strictly require heavy custom code; it can involve no-code solutions or human-in-the-loop automation, as long as it gets a working solution to your audience.
